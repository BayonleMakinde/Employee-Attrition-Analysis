# ⚒️ Data Forge Challenge #003

## Employee Attrition Analysis

## About the Challenge

Welcome to the third **Data Forge Weekly Challenge**.

For this challenge, you'll work with an employee dataset and investigate the patterns that may be associated with employee attrition.

Your task is to **explore the data, find meaningful patterns, communicate your findings, and provide useful recommendations based on the data.**

Whether you're a beginner, intermediate, or advanced data analyst, **you can participate.**

---

## 📊 Dataset Overview

The **Employee & Attrition Records Dataset** contains **1,000 employee records** designed to represent fictional workplace data.

The dataset contains information about:

- Employee demographics
- Departments and job roles
- Cities
- Date of joining
- Years spent at the company
- Monthly salary
- Overtime
- Job satisfaction
- Performance ratings
- Employee attrition

### Dataset Variables

| Variable | Description |
|---|---|
| `emp_id` | Unique identifier assigned to each employee |
| `full_name` | Fictional employee name |
| `gender` | Gender recorded for the employee |
| `age` | Employee's age |
| `department` | Department where the employee works |
| `job_role` | Employee's job role |
| `city` | City associated with the employee's record |
| `date_of_joining` | Date the employee joined the company |
| `years_at_company` | Number of years the employee has spent at the company |
| `monthly_salary` | Employee's monthly salary |
| `overtime` | Indicates whether the employee works overtime |
| `job_satisfaction` | Recorded job satisfaction score |
| `performance_rating` | Employee's performance rating |
| `attrition` | Indicates whether the employee left the company (`Yes` or `No`) |

---

## 💼 Business Problem

A company is experiencing employee turnover and wants to understand the patterns associated with employees leaving.

Management wants to understand:

> **What patterns are associated with employee attrition, and what can the company learn from its employee data to improve retention?**

You have been brought in as a data analyst to investigate the available employee records.

Your analysis should help the company understand its workforce, identify patterns that may be associated with attrition, and highlight areas that may require further investigation.

---

## 🔎 Your Task

Explore the dataset and use your findings to investigate employee attrition.

There is **no single correct approach** to this challenge.

You are encouraged to ask your own questions and investigate patterns that interest you.

### 🌱 If You're a Beginner

You can start with questions such as:

- How many employees are represented in the dataset?
- How many employees left the company, and how many stayed?
- What is the overall employee attrition rate?
- Which department has the highest number of employees?
- What is the average monthly salary?
- What is the distribution of job satisfaction scores?
- How many employees work overtime?

### 📈 If You're Intermediate

You can investigate questions such as:

- Which department has the highest attrition rate?
- How does attrition vary across job roles?
- Is there a difference in attrition rates between employees who work overtime and those who do not?
- How does job satisfaction relate to attrition?
- Do employees who have spent fewer years at the company show different attrition patterns?
- How does attrition vary across salary ranges?
- Is there a relationship between performance ratings and attrition?
- Are there noticeable differences in attrition across age groups?

### 🚀 If You're Advanced

You can take the analysis further by exploring:

- Relationships between multiple employee characteristics and attrition
- Whether job satisfaction, overtime, salary, and tenure jointly distinguish employees who left from those who stayed
- Differences between attrition rates and attrition counts across departments and job roles
- Statistical tests to investigate associations between categorical variables and attrition
- Correlations between numerical variables
- Employee segmentation or clustering
- Predictive modelling to estimate attrition risk
- Other patterns you discover independently

**You do not have to answer every question listed above.**

The questions are provided as starting points. We want to see your ability to **ask questions, investigate the data, and find the story behind it.**

Remember that patterns and associations do not necessarily establish the reasons employees leave.
